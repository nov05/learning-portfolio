# Project Overview

## What You’re Doing in Project 1 — and Why It Matters

When people hear "venture capital," they imagine complex finance and intimidating jargon. It's easy to think: *I'm not ready for that.*

Project 1 is here to prove the opposite.

In this project, you're not doing spreadsheets or valuations. You're doing the first thing every VC analyst learns: understand what a fund cares about, and find real startups that match that vision.

You won't be doing it alone. Each step is broken down clearly, AI is available at every stage, and by the end, you'll have something concrete: three thesis-fit startups and one you're ready to go deeper on in Project 2.

Get through Project 1 and you'll cross a real line—from *"VC sounds interesting"* to *"I've sourced a startup the way analysts actually do it."*
