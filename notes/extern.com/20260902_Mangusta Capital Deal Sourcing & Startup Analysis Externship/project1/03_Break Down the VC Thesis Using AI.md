# Break Down the VC Thesis Using AI

*Estimated Time to Complete the Task: 10 mins*

Investment theses are dense by design — they're internal documents written for people who already speak the language. Your job in this task is to change that. Use AI to break it down until the logic is clear enough to explain out loud. That's what analysts do before they use a thesis to make real decisions.

## Follow the Steps Below

* **Open an AI tool**

  * ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, or any other tool you prefer.

* **Paste the Mangusta Capital investment thesis into the chat**

  * Ask the AI to explain it in simple, clear language. We've shared some sample prompts below to help you.

  https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lXI9pdFOntBlNZbMslqIyBS5x2KBIRK7xJKxtCRBquo

* **Review what the AI gives you**

  * Does it make more sense now?
  * What feels clearer?
  * What’s still confusing?

* **Ask follow-up questions**

  * Keep re-prompting until the thesis feels easy to understand.

## Prompt Starter Kit

Good prompts usually include:

* **Set a persona**

  * e.g., “You are a VC analyst who explains things simply.”

* **Give background**

  * “This is the investment thesis from Mangusta Capital.”

* **Clarify what you want**

  * Simplify, summarize, and explain in plain language.

* **Choose an audience**

  * “Explain this to a high school student.”

Once you can explain the thesis in plain language, you're ready to use it. That's what Step 2 is for.

# Mangusta Capital’s Investment Thesis — Explained Simply

Mangusta believes the next generation of big technology companies will come from **AI tools built for specific industries**.

Instead of building a general-purpose AI tool like ChatGPT, Mangusta looks for startups that use AI to solve specific problems in industries such as finance, recruiting, or sales.

Think of it this way:

> **ChatGPT is like a general toolbox. Vertical AI is like a specialized machine built for one specific job.**

## Why Vertical AI?

Technology has followed this pattern before.

Early on, companies compete to build basic technology and infrastructure. Over time, that technology becomes cheaper and more widely available. The bigger opportunities then move toward software built for specific industries and customers.

Mangusta believes AI is following a similar path.

Companies such as **OpenAI, Anthropic, and DeepSeek** are competing to provide increasingly powerful AI models at lower costs. As these models become more widely available, the models themselves may become less differentiated.

That creates an opportunity for companies that build **useful, specialized products on top of these models**.

For example, instead of simply providing an AI model, a startup might build an AI system specifically for accountants, recruiters, doctors, insurance companies, or financial professionals. The startup understands the industry's workflow, connects to existing systems, and uses industry-specific data to solve problems that a general AI tool cannot easily handle.

## What Mangusta Looks For

Mangusta is **sector-agnostic**, meaning it is open to companies across many industries. It believes there will be major AI winners across many different sectors.

Some areas of interest include:

* **Fintech**
* **HR and recruiting**
* **CRM and sales development**

## When Does Mangusta Invest?

Mangusta primarily invests at the **Seed and Series A** stages.

These are relatively early stages, when a startup has developed its product and is beginning to show that customers want it.

Mangusta can also invest a little earlier or later when appropriate.

## What Makes a Startup Attractive?

When evaluating a startup, Mangusta looks for several important advantages:

### Proprietary Data

The company has valuable data that competitors cannot easily obtain or copy.

### First-Mover Advantage

The company gets an early lead in its market and has a chance to become the industry standard.

### Seamless Integrations

The product works smoothly with the software and systems customers already use.

## Where Does Mangusta Invest?

Mangusta prefers **U.S.-based companies**, but also considers promising startups from **Europe and Asia**.

## The Big Idea

Mangusta believes AI can dramatically improve productivity, especially in complicated industries where people still spend a lot of time on repetitive or inefficient work.

The opportunity is not simply to build another general-purpose AI tool. The bigger opportunity is to take powerful AI technology and turn it into a **specialized product that understands a particular industry's data, workflow, and problems**.

In simple terms:

**Build AI for a specific industry → solve a difficult problem really well → become deeply embedded in that industry → expand into more workflows → potentially become the industry's standard software platform.**
