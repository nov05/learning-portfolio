## Mangusta Capital’s Investment Thesis

Mangusta Capital is a venture capital fund launched by a former SoftBank Vision Fund leader and backed by the family behind Ray-Ban and Oakley. Its focus is finding and backing the next generation of startups.

For this externship, the focus is on startups building **Vertical AI**—sector-specific AI tools designed for a single industry, rather than general-purpose tools like ChatGPT.
