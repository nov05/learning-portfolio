## 📘 What Is an Investment Memo?

An **investment memo** is an internal document written by a VC firm to evaluate and advocate for a specific investment opportunity. It answers critical questions like:

* Why this startup?
* Why now?
* What’s the upside?
* What are the risks—and how do we mitigate them?
* How does this align with our fund’s thesis?

It's the *make-or-break* document that partners use to debate, stress-test, and ultimately decide whether or not to invest millions.
