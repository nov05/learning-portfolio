Each one sounds promising. Each one says they’ll revolutionize an industry. So, how does Mangusta figure out which ones are actually worth a closer look?

Of course, you can evaluate the basics—product quality, MVP strength, traction—but Mangusta uses something more strategic to filter fast.

That’s where their **four core investment themes** come in.

## 🧠 So, What’s a Theme?

Think of it like this:

Imagine you're building a fund focused on the future of education. Instead of backing any random “edtech” company, you might notice a pattern:

> “Startups using AI tutors to personalize learning are growing faster and retaining users longer.”

That pattern becomes your **theme**—AI-powered personalized learning.

Now, whenever a new education startup shows up, you ask: *Does this fit our theme?* If yes, it goes to the top of the pile. If not, maybe it’s not for you.

VCs use themes to focus their bets. It helps them filter noise and spot what’s *working at scale*. Mangusta does the same, but for **Vertical AI**. It has four themes it believes point to the most promising startups. You'll learn those next.

## The 4 Themes Mangusta Uses to Spot Winners

Here are the four themes Mangusta uses to cut through the noise and identify real opportunities in Vertical AI.

```text
1. AI-Powered Workflows

AI can handle tasks like drafting documents, processing claims, or triaging emails—especially in industries that rely on repetitive “knowledge work.”

Imagine a startup that builds an AI assistant for lawyers. It drafts legal documents, checks for errors, and organizes case files automatically. Instead of spending 3 hours writing a contract, a lawyer now clicks a button and reviews it in 10 minutes. That’s AI powering the workflow.

Why Mangusta cares:

These automations massively boost productivity. It’s like giving every worker a super-efficient assistant—at scale. For startups, this means real ROI and faster adoption.

2. Cost vs. Quality

AI tools today aren’t just fast—they’re also precise. And they’re often cheaper than human labor.

Think of a hospital using an AI tool that scans X-rays faster and more accurately than a junior radiologist—at one-fourth the cost. The hospital gets better outcomes and saves money. That’s a win-win, and it’s why customers keep paying for the tool.

Why Mangusta cares:

When you offer better results for less money, users stick around longer and spend more. This leads to stronger metrics like retention and lifetime value—exactly what investors look for.

3. Scalability

Great Vertical AI tools start with one use case but quickly expand across the customer’s entire workflow (and even industry).

A startup starts by helping dentists send appointment reminders with AI. But soon, clinics use it for billing, follow-ups, reviews, and even insurance claims. One small use case turned into a platform that handles the entire patient journey. That’s how startups scale fast.

Why Mangusta cares:

This creates Total Addressable Market (TAM) expansion opportunities. More users, more products, more revenue. And Mangusta loves startups that can scale this way.

4. Platform Power

When a startup becomes the go-to tool for an industry, it accumulates valuable data. It understands customers better—and builds tailored features faster.

Imagine an AI tool used by logistics companies to track packages. Over time, it learns from millions of deliveries and becomes insanely good at predicting delays or route changes. No competitor has that data. Customers stick to it because it just works better. The startup becomes the go-to platform for logistics AI.

Why Mangusta cares:

This creates defensibility. The product becomes hard to rip out, and competitors struggle to catch up. The startup becomes a true platform—not just a tool.
```

**This is where the bigger picture starts to come together.**

Now you’re beginning to understand how a **VC fund like Mangusta** doesn’t just chase trends—it thoughtfully designs where to invest and where to say no. This kind of strategic clarity is what sets great funds apart.
