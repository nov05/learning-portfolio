# How to Evaluate Startup Fit Quickly

*Estimated Time to Read: 5 mins*

## Mangusta Capital Thesis-Fit Checklist

Use this checklist while sourcing startups for Project 1.

**Location**

* U.S.-based preferred
* Europe and Asia may also be considered

**Stage**

* Seed or Series A preferred
* Pre-Seed or Series B acceptable if highly compelling

**Focus**

* AI tools at the application layer — **Vertical AI**
* Not general-purpose AI such as ChatGPT, Anthropic, or DeepSeek

**Target Sectors**
The startup should fit at least one of these sectors:

* Fintech
* HR / Recruiting / CRM / SDR

## Quick Filter

**U.S. + Seed/Series A + Vertical AI + Fintech or HR/Recruiting/CRM/SDR**

If a startup fails one of these filters, it is generally not a strong thesis fit for Project 1.
