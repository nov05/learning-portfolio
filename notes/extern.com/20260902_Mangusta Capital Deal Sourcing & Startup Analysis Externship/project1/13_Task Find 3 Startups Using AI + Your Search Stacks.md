# Task: Find 3 Startups Using AI + Your Search Stacks

*Estimated Time to Complete: 30 mins–2 hrs*

This is your first real startup search. You have the thesis. You have the tools. Now use them.

Your goal is to find **3 startups that appear to fit Mangusta Capital's investment criteria**. You're not making a final investment decision yet—that comes in Step 3. Right now, you're building the candidate list that your eventual investment recommendation will come from.

> **Note:** Sometimes you may not be able to find three startups on your first attempt. If you can only find one or two, that's completely okay—submit what you found.

## Important Selection Rules

### 🚫 Don't Pick Companies That...

* Are **too big or too late-stage**: Series C+, public companies, or already acquired.
* Are **general-purpose AI tools**, such as ChatGPT.

### ✅ Focus on Startups That...

* Are at the **Seed or Series A stage**. Pre-Seed or Series B companies are acceptable only if highly compelling.
* Are building **Vertical AI**: sector-specific AI at the application layer, rather than general-purpose AI tools.

## Follow the Steps Below

### 1. Use AI to Scout Startups

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Gemini to:

* Ask AI to suggest Vertical AI startups in your chosen sector, such as:

  * Fintech
  * HR / Recruiting
  * CRM / SDR
* Ask AI whether each startup fits the Mangusta thesis.

This should give you your first list of candidates.

### 2. Use Your Core Search Stack to Find More Startups

Use these sources after AI or in parallel. They can help you find startups that AI may miss:

* StartupHub.ai
* University accelerators and incubators
* YC Startup Directory
* Techstars
* 500 Global
* Plug and Play
* StartX
* MassChallenge
* LinkedIn search filters and founder posts
* YouTube demo videos

### 3. If You Still Can't Find Enough Startups, Use the Secondary Stack

Try:

* Google Search
* Reddit communities, such as r/startups and r/entrepreneur
* Product Hunt keyword search
* Wellfound, using Discover, filters, and keyword search
* Twitter/X, including hashtags such as #startups

### 4. Create a New Google Sheet with 3 Startups

Here is how your sheet should look:  
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/18Hu1lvkdiz7LN-wMXB85SCQEERVp_b7sCjGINvLLOu8

Create a new Google Sheet and add the following columns:

| Column                    | What to Include                                        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Organization Name**     | The official name of the startup                       |
| **Company Website URL**   | The startup's website                                  |
| **Amount Raised**         | Total funding raised so far                            |
| **Description**           | A brief overview of what the company does              |
| **Last Funding Date**     | The most recent funding date                           |
| **Last Funding Type**     | Seed, Series A, Series B, etc.                         |
| **Number of Employees**   | Current workforce size                                 |
| **Industries**            | Relevant sectors or industries, such as AI or FinTech  |
| **Headquarters Location** | City and/or country where the company is headquartered |

## Submit Your Work

1. In the upper-right corner of your Google Sheet, click **Share**.
2. Under **Get Link**, change the access setting to **Anyone with the link can view**.
3. Copy the Google Sheet link.
4. Paste the link into the submission box.

## How to Find Funding Date & Funding Type

### Using Crunchbase

1. Go to Crunchbase. https://www.crunchbase.com  
2. Search for the startup name.
3. Scroll to **Funding Rounds**.

There you'll find:

* **Last Funding Date**
* **Last Funding Type** — Seed, Series A, etc.
* **Amount Raised**

If Crunchbase blocks your view, try:

* Opening Crunchbase in Incognito Mode.
* Asking Perplexity.  

Sample prompt:

> "Find the latest funding round for <startup name>. Include the date, round type, and the credible source you used."

Your shortlist is the foundation of everything that follows. **Step 3 is where you narrow it down to one.**
