# Quick House Rules

*Following these keeps the externship running smoothly for you and future externs.*

Think of these as the **ground rules we all agree to**, so everyone can have a great experience and our partners keep coming back year after year.

They’re not here to dull your shine—just to make sure we protect the trust we’ve built with companies like Mangusta Capital.

By following them, you’re helping make this program the kind of opportunity future-you will be proud you were part of.

## Do

* ✅ Follow the suggested pacing to stay on track and meet deadlines.
* ✅ Join company meetings and Program Manager check-ins.
* ✅ Use Slack or your Program Manager for all questions and updates.
* ✅ Submit work that meets the quality standards outlined in the training modules.
* ✅ Follow the official guidance when posting about or showcasing your externship, including how it’s listed on your resume and LinkedIn.

## Don’t

* 🚫 Contact company executives, managers, or other employees directly unless invited through official channels.
* 🚫 Ask company representatives for jobs, internships, or referrals.
* 🚫 Send unsolicited emails, messages, or connection requests to anyone at the host company.
* 🚫 Misrepresent your role—you are an **extern** hosted by Extern, Inc. in partnership with the company, **not an intern or employee**.
* 🚫 List the company as your employer on LinkedIn or your résumé. Follow the instructions provided in training.
* 🚫 Share confidential project information, data, or deliverables outside approved channels.

## Why This Matters

When everyone follows these guidelines, our partnerships stay strong and the externship continues to be a trusted opportunity for current and future participants.

**Not following the guidelines can lead to lost opportunities for you and future externs. Our partners rely on your professionalism.**
