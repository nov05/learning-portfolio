# Know Your Host Company and Business Challenge

## Mangusta Capital

[Mangusta Capital](https://www.mangustacap.com/) is a venture capital fund that invests early in startups working on **AI, fintech, healthcare, and more**. They were one of the early backers of **xAI**, Elon Musk’s company building Grok to compete with OpenAI.

### Your Mission

**Act like a venture analyst and find the hidden gems—early-stage startups with bold ideas and big impact.**

💡 In a world powered by AI across every industry, the next big breakthrough might come from a small startup—and you might be the one to spot it.

Over this externship, you’ll work through **projects that stack your skills**, like tracks on an album.

### 🎯 Project Themes

* **Project 1:** Source startups and identify early-stage opportunities
* **Project 2:** Conduct deep-dive due diligence across market, team, technology, and competitors
* **Project 3:** Turn diligence data into actionable insights and synergy analysis
* **Project 4:** Craft a clear, concise investment memo to make the case for funding

🔥 Along the way, you’ll learn how to enhance your output and workflow with cutting-edge AI tools.

# Meet Your Company Guide: Natoli Farber

**Natoli is a Principal at Mangusta Capital**, where he focuses on sourcing, evaluating, and supporting early-stage vertical AI startups.

Before joining Mangusta, he worked at **Ligo Partners**, a single-family office that invests alongside a trusted group of investors in cutting-edge tech startups backed by top venture capital firms.

He holds a **Bachelor of Science from Wake Forest University**.

Natoli is excited to work with externs who are curious, thoughtful, and ready to explore how top VC funds like Mangusta identify and back the next wave of industry-specific AI startups.
