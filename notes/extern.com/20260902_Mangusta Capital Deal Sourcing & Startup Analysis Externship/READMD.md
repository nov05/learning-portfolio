**Project 1: Sourcing Startups**  

Deal flow is the foundation of every VC fund — and it starts with someone who knows what to look for. In this project, you'll do what junior analysts at real venture funds do: learn what Mangusta Capital is looking for, go find it, and make the case for why one startup deserves a closer look. By the end, you'll have a shortlisted company ready for deep due diligence — and your first real analyst output to show for it.


- Step 1: Understand the Investment Thesis

    Objective

    Mangusta Capital sees hundreds of startups a year. Most don't make it past the first conversation. In this step, you'll learn exactly why—by getting inside the investment thesis that drives every decision they make. By the end, you'll know how to look at a startup the way they do.

    - Step Inside a Real Investment Memo (Optional)
    - Project Overview
    - Overview of Your VC Journey
    - Mangusta Capital Investment Thesis
    - Break Down the VC Thesis Using AI

    - Task: Explore the Vertical AI Deck from Mangusta Capital (Optional)

- Step 2: Source Three Startups that Match Investment Thesis

    Objective   
    This is where the sourcing begins. You'll use the same search methods VC analysts use — AI tools, startup databases, and secondary research — to find three startups that fit Mangusta Capital's thesis. The shortlist you build here feeds directly into Step 3, and one of those companies will carry through to your final investment memo. Choose well.

    - How to Evaluate Startup Fit Quickly
    - How to Find Startups Using AI
