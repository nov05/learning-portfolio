# Share Your Work

**Estimated time to complete the task: 5 minutes (after production)**

Congratulations! You’ve created a powerful summary of your work, in an effective structure designed for making people hear your ideas. Submit your video or slides in this task. They're your Week 1 deliverable demonstrating your ability to analyze corporate failures using the four-lens framework.

## Steps to Complete the Task

### Step 1

Verify your diagnosis includes all requirements before uploading.

**Video/Carousel Content Requirements**

* **Company name** is clearly stated
* **Corruption event** is specific (date, what happened)
* **One lens** is identified as the primary failure
* **Evidence** supports your diagnosis
* **Structural lesson** generalizes beyond this company

**Video Requirements**

* Length: 60–90 seconds
* Audio is clear and understandable
* Captions are included (accessibility + sound-off viewing)
* Video is stable (not shaky)

**Carousel Requirements**

* 3–4 slides
* Text is readable on mobile
* Each slide makes sense on its own
* Your name appears on the final slide

## What Makes a Strong Diagnostic

🔥 **Heads up:** The program manager may curate 3–5 favorites from each cohort for potential endorsement from Eric.

Here's what stands out:

| Element       | What Makes It Strong                                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Hook**      | Provocative, specific, not generic                     |
| **Diagnosis** | Commits to one lens, doesn't hedge                     |
| **Evidence**  | Concrete, verifiable, well-chosen                      |
| **Lesson**    | Generalizable, not just "they should have been better" |
| **Delivery**  | Clear, confident, professional                         |

### Example vs. Non-Example

| **Strong**                                                                                              | **Weak**                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Governance failed because the founder had voting control but used it for extraction, not protection."  | "Multiple things went wrong including bad leadership and market conditions." |
| "Dual-class shares only protect missions when the controlling shareholder is aligned with the mission." | "Companies should be more careful."                                          |
