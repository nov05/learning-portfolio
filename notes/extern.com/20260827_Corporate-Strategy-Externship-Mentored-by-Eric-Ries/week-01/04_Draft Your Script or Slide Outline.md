# Draft Your Script or Slide Outline

*Estimated time: 25 minutes*

Transform your Incorruptible scorecard into a shareable format.

Choose one:

* **Video Script:** 60–90 second TikTok/Reel/Short
* **Carousel Slides:** 3–4 slides for LinkedIn/Instagram

Both formats use the same four beats.

## Step 1 — Choose Your Format

| Format              | Best For                             | Output          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| **Video Script**    | On-camera, dramatic stories          | 60–90 sec video |
| **Carousel Slides** | Visual thinkers, data-heavy analyses | 3–4 slides      |

## Step 2 — Pull from Your Scorecard

Include:

* Company + corruption event
* Primary failure lens + evidence
* Structural lesson

## Step 3 — Write Your Hook

The hook is the most important line.

**Strong:**

* “WeWork didn't fail because Adam Neumann was weird.”
* “Private equity killed Toys R Us, then blamed Amazon.”
* “Kraft promised to save British chocolate. They lied.”

**Weak:**

* “Let me tell you about WeWork.”
* “Here's what happened to Toys R Us.”
* “Cadbury is an interesting case.”

## Step 4 — Use the Four Beats

1. **Mission** — What was the company supposed to be?
2. **Corruption Event** — What specific event changed everything?
3. **Diagnosis** — Which lens failed, and what evidence supports it?
4. **Lesson** — What structural lesson generalizes?

## Step 5 — Measure Your Draft

* **Video:** Read aloud; target 60–90 seconds.
* **Carousel:** Each slide should take 5–10 seconds to read.

## Video Script Example — WeWork

**OPENING:**
“WeWork didn't fail because Adam Neumann was weird. It failed because its mission was a lie.”

WeWork was supposed to build community through shared workspaces.

**THE CORRUPTION EVENT:**
In September 2019, the IPO filing revealed the truth: $1.9 billion in losses and the CEO selling his own trademarks back to the company.

**THE DIAGNOSIS:**
The core failure was mission drive. WeWork could get rich while its members got overpriced desks. There was zero structural connection between purpose and profit.

**THE LESSON:**
If your mission is marketing and not mechanics, financial gravity will eventually expose the gap. WeWork didn't need a different CEO. It needed a business where success required delivering on its promises.

## Strong Draft vs. Weak Draft

| Strong Draft                | Weak Draft                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Provocative hook            | “Let me tell you about...”       |
| Specific date and parties   | Vague timeline                   |
| One root-cause lens         | Lists every problem              |
| Evidence supports diagnosis | Interesting but irrelevant facts |
| Generalizable lesson        | “It's complicated”               |

## What You Should Have

* A complete first draft
* All four beats
* A strong hook
* Content ready for peer feedback
