# Identify the Pattern

**Estimated time:** 10 minutes

You've seen two companies from different industries, countries, and eras suffer the same fate. Analyze them together to identify the structural vulnerability they shared.

## Step 1: Compare the Companies

| Company | Industry       | Country | Era  | Outcome                                |
| ------- | -------------- | ------- | ---- | -------------------------------------- |
| FedMart | Retail         | USA     | 1975 | Founder ousted, company died           |
| Cadbury | Consumer goods | UK      | 2010 | Hostile acquisition, mission corrupted |

Both were mission-driven and successful before eventually being corrupted or destroyed.

## Step 2: Identify the Shared Vulnerability

* Both were publicly traded
* Both had founders/families strongly committed to the mission
* Both lacked structural protection against hostile takeovers
* Both were attractive targets because of their success

## Step 3: Consider Possible Governance Protections

* Foundation ownership, such as Novo Nordisk
* Dual-class shares with mission-aligned voting control
* Poison pills or supermajority requirements
* Public Benefit Corporation status

## Step 4: Written Response

**Question:** Which structural feature did FedMart and Cadbury share that made them vulnerable to the same fate?

Your response should:

1. Name the specific structural vulnerability
2. Explain why it made both companies vulnerable
3. Connect it to the four-lens framework and identify which lens failed
4. Reference evidence from both cases

A strong response should connect the structural vulnerability to the outcomes rather than simply restating facts.

## Why This Matters

The same pattern appears in more recent cases:

| Company     | Year | What Happened                                                                   |
| ----------- | ---: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Twitter     | 2022 | Public company acquired by a single buyer who changed the company substantially |
| Whole Foods | 2017 | Amazon acquisition changed company culture and priorities                       |
| LinkedIn    | 2016 | Microsoft acquisition shifted the company's focus                               |

The pattern to look for is:

**Strong mission + weak governance → vulnerability to external control**
