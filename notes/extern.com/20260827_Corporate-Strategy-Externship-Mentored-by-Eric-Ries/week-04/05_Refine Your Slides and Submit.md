# Section 1: Refine Your Introduction (Slide 1)

## Quality Check

Read your introduction slide aloud. Does it sound like you, or like a generic AI response?

| Element                 | Strong                                   | Weak                                |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Context line**        | "Senior at UCLA studying Economics"      | "Student interested in business"    |
| **Personal connection** | Specific story or observation            | "I wanted to learn about companies" |
| **Authenticity**        | Sounds like something you'd actually say | Sounds corporate or generic         |

## Refinement Questions

Ask yourself:

* **Is this actually true?** Don't use a story the AI invented. Use your real experience.
* **Would I say this to a friend?** If it sounds too formal, rewrite it.
* **Does it connect to the Incorruptible theme?** Your reason for being here should relate to mission alignment.

## Common Fixes

| AI Gave You                                     | Refine To                             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| "I'm passionate about corporate responsibility" | Your actual reason for enrolling      |
| Generic career goals                            | A specific experience or observation  |
| Overly formal language                          | How you'd actually introduce yourself |

# Section 2: Refine Your Company Slide (Slide 2)

## Quality Check

Does your company slide explain why *you* chose this company, not just what the company does?

| Element              | Strong                                    | Weak                                |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Description**      | Clear, 1-sentence explanation             | Company history dump                |
| **Selection reason** | Personal, specific, reveals your thinking | "I thought it would be interesting" |
| **Context**          | Timely, relevant details                  | Missing or generic                  |

## Refinement Questions

* **Is the description accurate?** Verify against the company's actual business.
* **Is your reason genuine?** Use your real reason, not what sounds impressive.
* **Did you include relevant context?** Recent news, IPO, controversy, personal connection?

## Common Fixes

| AI Gave You                              | Refine To                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Wikipedia-style company overview         | One-sentence description              |
| Generic "mission-driven company" framing | Your specific interest or concern     |
| Missing context                          | Add recent news or personal relevance |

# Section 3: Refine Your Scorecard (Slide 3)

## Quality Check

This is the analytical core. Your grades and findings must be specific and defensible.

| Element       | Strong                   | Weak                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| **Rating**    | Committed (4, not 3.25)  | Hedged or all the same |
| **Findings**  | Specific evidence cited  | Vague descriptions     |
| **Synthesis** | Clear pattern identified | Generic conclusion     |

## Refinement Questions

For each lens:

* **Can I defend this rating?** If Eric asks "why a 3?", do you have an answer?
* **Is the finding specific?** Could someone understand the rating from the finding alone?
* **Is this my analysis or the AI's?** Make sure it reflects your actual research.

## Common Fixes

| AI Gave You                                 | Refine To                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Governance: 3 — Good governance practices" | "Governance: 3 — No dual-class shares; activist investors own 11%" |
| "Overall they seem mission-aligned"         | Specific pattern: "Strong intent, weak protection"                 |

> 🎯 **The scorecard is where Eric assesses your analytical rigor.** Vague findings signal shallow analysis. Specific evidence signals mastery.

# Section 4: Refine Your Action (Slide 4)

## Quality Check

Your action must be specific and implementable—not a wish or a vague directive.

| Element               | Strong                      | Weak                       |
| --------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Lens choice**       | Justified (why this one?)   | Random or easiest          |
| **Problem statement** | Connects to your analysis   | Generic concern            |
| **Action**            | Specific, concrete, doable  | "Improve governance"       |
| **Evidence**          | Precedent or research cited | "This would probably work" |

## Refinement Questions

* **Did I choose the most important lens, not the easiest?** Eric will notice if you avoid the hard problem.
* **Is my action specific enough to implement?** "Adopt dual-class shares before next proxy vote" vs. "improve governance"
* **Do I have evidence it would work?** Cite Novo Nordisk, Patagonia, or research.

## Common Fixes

| AI Gave You                      | Refine To                                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Focus on improving governance"  | "Adopt a mission committee with veto power over strategic decisions"   |
| "Other companies have done this" | "Novo Nordisk's foundation blocked a hostile acquisition in the 2010s" |
| Missing implementation           | "Requires board approval before next proxy season"                     |
