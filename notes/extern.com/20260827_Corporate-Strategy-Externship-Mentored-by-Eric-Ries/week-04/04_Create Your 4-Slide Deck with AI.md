# Create Your 4-Slide Deck with AI

**Estimated time to complete the task:** 30 minutes

You'll use AI to generate a complete first draft of your 4-slide presentation. By providing your company analysis and personal context, you'll get a working deck that you can then refine.

---

## What You're Building

A complete 4-slide presentation draft covering:

1. **Introduction** — Who you are and why this matters to you
2. **Your Company** — The company you evaluated and why you chose it
3. **Evaluation Scorecard** — Your four-lens grades and key findings
4. **Proposed Action** — Which lens you'd improve and how

**Total presentation time target:** 3–4 minutes

---

## Before You Start

Gather these materials from your previous work:

* Your company name and what they do
* Your four-lens ratings (1–5)
* Key evidence for each grade
* Your proposed action and the lens it targets

> ⏳ Budget a little time to revisit your company's proxy statement, financials, or recent news before generating your slides.

---

# Steps to Complete the Task

## Step 1: Write Your AI Prompt

Copy and customize this prompt template with your information:

```text
I'm creating a 4-slide presentation about [COMPANY NAME] for an externship program on corporate mission alignment. Help me draft all 4 slides.

**About Me:**
- Name: [YOUR NAME]
- Context: [Your school/job/career direction - 1 line]
- Why I enrolled: [1-2 sentences about why corporate mission alignment matters to you personally]

**About My Company:**
- Company: [COMPANY NAME]
- What they do: [1 sentence description]
- Why I chose them: [Your reasoning - personal connection, curiosity, timing, etc.]
- Relevant context: [Any recent news, IPO, controversy, job offer, etc.]

**My Four-Lens Evaluation:**
- Mission Drive: [Rating 1-5] — [Key evidence]
- Financial Gravity: [Rating 1-5] — [Key evidence]
- Governance: [Rating 1-5] — [Key evidence]
- Mission Transmission: [Rating 1-5] — [Key evidence]
- Overall Rating: [1-5]
- Pattern I see: [1-2 sentences synthesizing your analysis]

**My Proposed Action:**
- Focus Lens: [Which lens you're targeting]
- The Problem: [Why this lens needs attention]
- Proposed Action: [Specific, concrete recommendation]
- Why it would work: [Evidence or precedent]

Please create 4 slides with:
1. Slide 1: Introduction (30 seconds speaking time)
2. Slide 2: My Company (45 seconds speaking time)
3. Slide 3: Evaluation Scorecard (60 seconds speaking time)
4. Slide 4: Proposed Action (45 seconds speaking time)

For each slide, give me:
- A title
- The key content (bullet points or short paragraphs)
- Speaker notes with what I should say
```

---

## Step 2: Fill In Your Details

Replace each bracketed placeholder with your actual information. Be specific—vague inputs produce vague outputs.

### Strong Input Examples

* "I interned at a 'purpose-driven' startup that fired half its team after funding. I want to understand why mission statements become lies."

* "Governance: D — No dual-class shares, no poison pill, and three activist-affiliated directors joined in 2023."

### Weak Input Examples

* "I'm interested in business"
* "Governance could be better"

---

## Step 3: Generate Your Draft

Submit your prompt to your AI tool of choice.

* **Canva AI** — Canva has a great AI tool that converts your slide ideas into beautiful presentations. Great for finding your personal style.
* **Claude AI** — You can copy your prompt directly into Claude to produce a simple set of slides that you can edit in Google Slides.
* **Gamma AI** — A powerful slide generation tool that creates highly polished slides.

---

## What the AI Will Give You

A good AI response will provide:

| Slide       | What You'll Get                                                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Slide 1** | Your name, context line, and personal connection statement          |
| **Slide 2** | Company description, your selection reasoning, and relevant context |
| **Slide 3** | Four-lens table with grades and findings, plus overall assessment   |
| **Slide 4** | Focus lens, problem statement, proposed action, and evidence        |

Plus speaker notes for each slide to guide your recording.
