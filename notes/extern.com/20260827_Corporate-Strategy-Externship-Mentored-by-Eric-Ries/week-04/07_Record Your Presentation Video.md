# Record Your Presentation Video

**Estimated time to complete:** 20 minutes

You'll record your 4-slide presentation, creating the video you'll submit and potentially present to Eric Ries in your final mentor session.

---

## What You're Creating

A 3–4 minute video of you presenting your 4-slide presentation. Eric will watch this recording, and you may also present it live in your mentor session.

| Requirement | Target                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Length**  | 3–4 minutes                     |
| **Format**  | Video (face + slides visible)   |
| **Audio**   | Clear, minimal background noise |
| **Slides**  | Readable throughout             |

---

## Steps to Complete the Task

### Step 1: Final Rehearsal

Run through your presentation one more time, out loud, before you start recording.

**Check:**

* Do I know each slide without reading?
* Am I hitting the 3–4 minute target?
* Do I have a clear anchor point for each slide?

### Step 2: Set Up Your Recording

**Technical checklist:**

* Recording tool open (Loom, Zoom, or other)
* Slides loaded and ready to share
* Camera at eye level
* Lighting on your face (not behind you)
* Audio source selected
* Quiet environment
* Test recording done

**Do a 30-second test recording.** Watch it back. Confirm that the audio is clear, the slides are readable, and you're well-lit.

### Step 3: Record

**Recording process:**

1. Start your recording tool.
2. Begin screen sharing with your slides.
3. Take a breath.
4. Present all four slides.
5. End with a clear close: "Thank you" or "I'm happy to take questions."
6. Stop recording.

**During recording:**

* Look at the camera, not the slides.
* Use natural pauses between slides.
* If you make a minor mistake, continue without comment.
* Only stop if there's a major technical failure or complete loss of thought.

### Step 4: Review Your Recording

Watch your recording once, all the way through.

**Checklist:**

| Quality Check                   | Pass? |
| ------------------------------- | ----- |
| Audio clear throughout          |       |
| Slides readable                 |       |
| Face visible and well-lit       |       |
| Length: 3–4 minutes             |       |
| No major technical issues       |       |
| Content complete (all 4 slides) |       |

### Step 5: Decide: Keep or Re-record

**Keep your recording if:**

* It meets all technical requirements.
* Content is complete.
* Minor imperfections don't affect clarity.

**Re-record if:**

* Audio is hard to understand.
* Slides are unreadable.
* There is a major technical failure.
* The recording is significantly over 5 minutes.
* You stumbled badly in the first 30 seconds.

**One re-record maximum.** If your second take has minor issues, submit it anyway. Over-perfecting leads to diminishing returns.

### Step 6: Export and Save

**File requirements:**

* **Format:** MP4, MOV, or Loom link
* **Name:** `[YourName]-Incorruptible-Presentation.mp4`
* **Storage:** Save it somewhere you can access for submission.

**If using Loom:**

* Copy the share link.
* Set visibility to **"Anyone with the link."**
* Save the link for your submission.
