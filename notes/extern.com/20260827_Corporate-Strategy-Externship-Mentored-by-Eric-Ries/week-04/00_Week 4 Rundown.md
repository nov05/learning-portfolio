## The 4-Slide Framework

| Slide                       | Purpose                       | Key Question                                             |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Slide 1: Introduction**   | Who you are                   | Who am I, and why does this matter to me?                |
| **Slide 2: Your Company**   | Company you evaluated + why   | What company did I analyze, and why?                     |
| **Slide 3: Your Scorecard** | Four-lens evaluation findings | What did my analysis reveal?                             |
| **Slide 4: Your Action**    | Lens to improve + proposal    | What would I do to make this company more incorruptible? |
