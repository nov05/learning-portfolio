
# Pick Your Patient

**Estimated time to complete the task: 10 minutes**

Pick a company that gives you the Ick Factor. By Week 1's end, you'll create a brief video (60-90 second) or mini slide deck (3-4 slides) explaining what killed or corrupted it.

**Hot tip:** The best diagnoses come from companies you have feelings about. Don't pick neutral—pick something that surprised, angered, or confused you.

## Steps to Complete the Task

### Step 1

Browse the company options below. Each category contains companies with different types of failures—tech collapses, consumer brand implosions, classic betrayals, and foundational case studies.

### Step 2

Pick ONE company that resonates with you. Consider:

* Do you have emotional investment in this company's story?
* Is there enough public information available (news, docs, podcasts, books)?
* Can you identify a clear corruption event (IPO, acquisition, founder ouster, etc.)?

### Step 3

If none of the listed companies work for you, you can propose your own—but it should meet all three criteria above.

### Step 4

Submit your company choice and a one-sentence explanation of why you picked it.

### Proposing Your Own Company

If you want to pitch a different company, it should meet ALL three criteria:

* It failed, drifted, or got corrupted — not just "struggling"
* Public info exists — news, docs, podcasts, books
* Clear corruption event — IPO, acquisition, founder ouster, etc.

## Example vs. Non-Example

Before you submit, check out what makes a strong pick vs. a weak one.

### Strong Picks

* **WeWork** — I watched the documentary and couldn't believe how obvious the red flags were
* **Etsy** — I'm a seller who watched the company change after IPO
* **Twitter** — I want to understand if better governance could've prevented it

### Weak Picks

* **Apple** — hasn't died or drifted, it's complicated
* **Random company I've never heard of** — no emotional investment = weak analysis
* **My friend's startup** — not enough public info
