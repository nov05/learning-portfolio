# The Life Pie Exercise

Estimated Reading Time: 15 minutes

Why this, why now?

You've learned what human flourishing means and ranked which Incorruptible lenses matter most to you. Now it's time to zoom out. Your career is one slice of a larger pie — and a company that scores well on your top lens can still harm your overall flourishing if it drains the other areas of your life.

What is the Life Pie?

The Life Pie is a self-reflection tool created by Janice Fraser to help you visualize eight key aspects of a fulfilling life. It answers a simple question: Where does my work feed and fuel me?

A common trap for ambitious people is optimizing for career success while neglecting everything else. The Life Pie helps you see the whole picture — where you are now, what you want from work, and where the gaps are.

Important: The goal is NOT to have all aspects of life fully nourished by work. This exercise helps you identify which aspects you want your work to fulfill — and by how much. Some areas may be better nourished by family, hobbies, or community.

The Eight Wedges

The Life Pie divides fulfillment into eight domains:

| Wedge                    | What It Covers                                             |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Financial Stability      | Income, savings, security, progress toward financial goals |
| Intellectual Fulfillment | Learning, growth, mental stimulation, creative challenge   |
| Physical Wellbeing       | Health, energy, fitness, sleep, body care                  |
| Emotional Wellbeing      | Mental health, inner peace, emotional resilience           |
| Spiritual Fulfillment    | Purpose, meaning, values, sense of contribution            |
| Loved Ones               | Family, close relationships, intimate connections          |
| Community                | Belonging, friendships, social connections, tribe          |
| Physical Surroundings    | Home, workspace, environment, the places you inhabit       |

Try it Out!

Open the Life Pie interactive tool and work through the four steps:

The Life Pie Tool: [https://life-pie-exercise.netlify.app/](https://life-pie-exercise.netlify.app/)

The exercise will guide you through:

1. What should your work nourish? — Select the areas you want your work to feed and fulfill.
2. What does your work currently nourish? — Select the areas your work is actually feeding right now.
3. How nourished is each area overall? — Rate each wedge considering all sources: work, family, hobbies, community, etc.
4. See your results — View gaps, alignments, and areas that need attention.

Take your time. Be honest. This is for you, not for anyone else.

When you finish, download or copy your results — you'll reference them in the next step.

Interpreting Your Results

Gaps to Address

These are areas you want your work to nourish, but it currently doesn't. This can mean:

* You're getting this nourishment elsewhere, such as family, hobbies, or community.
* It's an unmet need that you may want to address through your career choices.

Ask: "Is this gap okay because I get it elsewhere? Or is this something I need to find in my next role?"

Well Aligned

These are areas where your work is nourishing what you want it to nourish.

Ask: "What about my current or past work creates this alignment? How do I find that again?"

Lowest Nourished Areas

These wedges have the least fulfillment from all sources combined — work, family, hobbies, everything.

Ask: "Could work help fill these? Or do I need to find nourishment outside of work?"

The Career Connection

Based on your Life Pie results, answer these questions:

1. Which wedges should your career nourish?

List the 2–3 wedges you selected in Step 1 of the exercise.

2. Where are your biggest gaps?

List any wedges that you want work to nourish, but it currently doesn't.

3. What does this mean for your company evaluation?

| Life Pie Wedge           | How Company Quality Affects It                                          |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Financial Stability      | Governance protects your equity; financial gravity affects job security |
| Intellectual Fulfillment | Mission-driven companies often provide more meaningful challenges       |
| Physical Wellbeing       | Companies under pressure often burn out employees                       |
| Emotional Wellbeing      | Sustainable culture vs. chronic stress                                  |
| Spiritual Fulfillment    | Strong mission alignment can make work feel purposeful                  |
| Loved Ones               | Whether you have time and energy left for relationships                 |
| Community                | Whether colleagues become friends vs. competitors                       |
| Physical Surroundings    | Remote work flexibility, office quality, location                       |

The four lenses don't just predict company success. They also help predict whether working there will support or drain your Life Pie.

What Do You Actually Want From Work?

Based on your Life Pie, complete this statement:

"The most important thing I want from my career right now is:

---

Because it will help me nourish my:

---

And I'm willing to accept less nourishment in:"

Example:

"The most important thing I want from my career right now is intellectual challenge without burnout. Because it will help me nourish my Intellectual Fulfillment without draining my Physical and Emotional Wellbeing. And I'm willing to accept less nourishment in Financial Stability, as long as I have enough for security."

Your Human Flourishing Priorities

Which Life Pie wedges does your career most need to support?

Pick 2–3 wedges that are either low-scoring or high-stakes and can't afford to let them drop.

TL;DR

* The Life Pie divides fulfillment into eight domains: Financial Stability, Intellectual Fulfillment, Physical Wellbeing, Emotional Wellbeing, Spiritual Fulfillment, Loved Ones, Community, and Physical Surroundings.
* Use the interactive tool to identify what you want work to nourish vs. what it currently does.
* Gaps between "should" and "does" are opportunities — either to seek in your next role or to fill outside of work.
* The four lenses predict not just company success, but whether working there will support or drain your Life Pie.
* Your lens ranking + Life Pie = a complete picture of what you need from your next role.
