# Human Flourishing Connection

Estimated Reading Time: 10 minutes

Why This Matters

Eric Ries wrote Incorruptible not just to help companies stay successful, but because he believes how companies operate affects whether people can flourish. The four lenses are therefore not only corporate analysis tools; they can also help evaluate whether a workplace supports your own wellbeing and development.

Why “Flourishing” and Not Just “Success”?

Eric deliberately chose “flourishing” over success, happiness, or wealth because it captures something deeper.

“Flourishing” means developing your potential as a human being. Work that pays the bills but crushes your spirit is not flourishing. Work that is exciting but burns you out is not flourishing. Flourishing means that your work contributes to your growth while leaving room for other parts of life.

Flourishing involves:

* Growing, not stagnating
* Contributing, not just consuming
* Balancing multiple areas of life, not just one

The FedMart Standard

Sol Price, founder of FedMart, believed a company's purpose extended beyond making money. He believed companies should create conditions for human flourishing among employees, customers, and communities.

The Connection Between Company and Employee Flourishing

| If the Company...              | Then Employees...                                              |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Has strong Mission Drive       | Can find genuine meaning in their work                         |
| Manages Financial Gravity well | Aren't asked to compromise their values for short-term targets |
| Has solid Governance           | Can trust that good decisions will be protected                |
| Transmits Mission positively   | Can be proud of their employer's impact                        |

The reverse is also true:

| If the Company...                         | Then Employees...                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Is mission-hopeful but not mission-driven | Experience cognitive dissonance between values and reality |
| Bends to every financial pressure         | Burn out chasing unsustainable targets                     |
| Has weak governance                       | Watch helplessly as what they built gets corrupted         |
| Drags down industry standards             | Feel complicit in making the world worse                   |

This is why the four lenses matter for your career, not just for investors. A company that fails these tests may not only lose money; it can also cost employees years of their lives and undermine their sense of integrity.

The Human Flourishing Test

When evaluating your chosen company, ask:

“Will working at this company help me flourish, or will it extract value from me until I'm depleted?”

Companies that score poorly across the four lenses tend to be extractive: they hire talented people, use them up, and discard them. The company may flourish temporarily while the people inside it do not.

Companies that score well tend to be generative: they create conditions where people can grow, contribute, and maintain the rest of their lives.

Initial Reflection

Before diving into the specific frameworks, consider:

What does flourishing look like for you right now?

What would need to be true for you to say, “I'm thriving” in 2–3 years?

Beyond salary, what would a company need to provide for you to flourish there?

Think about meaning, growth, relationships, time, energy, and impact.

Hold onto these answers. You'll revisit them after completing the lens-ranking and Life Pie exercises, and your thinking may become more specific.

The Standard to Hold

“The question isn't just ‘Will this company succeed?’ The question is ‘Will I flourish while helping it succeed, and will I still flourish if it fails?’”

When evaluating your company, keep this standard in mind. A company can be successful and still be a terrible place to work. A company can be struggling and still be an incredible place to grow.

The four lenses help you see beyond the surface.

TL;DR

* Flourishing means the full development of human potential, not just success or happiness.
* Sol Price's FedMart standard: companies should create conditions for employee flourishing.
* A company's Incorruptible score can affect whether employees are able to flourish.
