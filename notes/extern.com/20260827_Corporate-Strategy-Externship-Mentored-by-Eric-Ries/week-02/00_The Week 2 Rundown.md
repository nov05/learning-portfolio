## Building on Week 1

Last week, you learned to diagnose failing companies using the four-lens framework. You saw how **strong mission + weak governance = vulnerability**.

Now you'll apply those same skills to a company you actually care about.

**Materials you'll need:**

* Understanding of the four lenses: Mission Drive, Financial Gravity, Governance, and Mission Transmission
* The FedMart and Cadbury patterns in mind
* Willingness to reflect honestly on your own values

## What You'll Do in Each Step

### Step 1: Find Your Fit

Before evaluating anyone else, start with yourself. Which of the four lenses matter most to you? What does fulfillment actually mean for your life?

**Includes:**

* Human Flourishing Connection — Eric's framework for why company quality affects you
* Which Lenses Matter Most to You — personal prioritization exercise
* The Life Pie Exercise — satisfaction across eight life domains
* Choose Your Company — selection task

### Step 2: Evaluate Your Company Across the Four Lenses

Apply the four-lens framework to a company you believe in or might work for. Research public information, evaluate each lens, and build a comprehensive Due Diligence Scorecard.

**Includes:**

* Mission Drive Analysis
* Financial Gravity Analysis
* Governance Analysis
* Mission Transmission Analysis
* Complete Your Due Diligence Scorecard

### Step 3: Share Your Findings & Ask Eric

Synthesize your analysis and prepare a question for Eric Ries that reflects your genuine curiosity about your company.

**Includes:**

* Share Your Analysis
* Ask Eric
