## What to Ask About

Now that you've moved from analyzing companies to proposing actual change, you might have questions such as:

* What do you do when the obvious **Transmission Multiplier** in an organization is actually part of the problem you're trying to fix?

* How do you rebuild a **Culture Bank** that has already been depleted? Is it possible to make new deposits when trust has been broken, or does meaningful change require new leadership?

* How do you stay in **"outsider mode"** long enough to push for change before becoming socialized into accepting the status quo?
