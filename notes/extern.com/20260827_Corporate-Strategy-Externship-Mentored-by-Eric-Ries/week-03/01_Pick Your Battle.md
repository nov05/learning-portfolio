## Part 2: Select Your Lens Focus

### The Decision

You have four options. Choose **ONE**.

| Lens                     | Focus Area                                 | Good Choice If...                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mission Drive**        | How deeply the mission motivates decisions | The company needs clearer mission integration, or has strong mission energy to amplify      |
| **Financial Gravity**    | Alignment between profit model and mission | There's tension between making money and mission, or an opportunity to strengthen alignment |
| **Governance**           | Structures that protect long-term mission  | Accountability gaps exist, or governance structures could be enhanced                       |
| **Mission Transmission** | How the mission spreads and evolves        | The mission isn't reaching all stakeholders, or transmission mechanisms could improve       |

### Decision Framework

#### Option 1: Follow the Gap

Choose your **weakest-rated lens**. Your proposal addresses a critical weakness.

**Pros:** High potential impact; addresses a real need
**Cons:** May face more resistance; harder to implement

**Best if:** The gap is significant enough that improvement would meaningfully change the company's trajectory.

#### Option 2: Amplify the Strength

Choose your **strongest-rated lens**. Your proposal makes a strength even stronger.

**Pros:** Builds on existing momentum; may be easier to implement
**Cons:** May have less transformative impact

**Best if:** The company has genuine strengths worth reinforcing and you can identify a specific way to amplify them.

#### Option 3: Follow Your Interest

Choose the lens that **most interests you**—where you have ideas, curiosity, or relevant experience.

**Pros:** Your engagement will come through in the proposal
**Cons:** May not be the highest-leverage choice

**Best if:** You have a specific idea that excites you and can make a compelling case for it.

#### Option 4: Follow the Path

Choose the lens where you see the **clearest path to action**—where a specific proposal is already forming.

**Pros:** Concrete proposals are more compelling
**Cons:** Clarity of path doesn't always mean highest impact

**Best if:** You've already started thinking about what you would propose.
