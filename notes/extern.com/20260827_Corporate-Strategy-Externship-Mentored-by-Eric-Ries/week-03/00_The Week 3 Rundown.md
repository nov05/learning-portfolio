## Choosing Your Lens: Two Strategies

### Strategy A: Strengthen a Bright Spot

Find a lens where the company scored well and propose an idea that makes it even stronger.

* Builds on existing momentum
* Often easier to implement (less resistance)
* Shows you understand what's working

**Example:** "Patagonia scores high on Mission Drive. My proposal strengthens this by creating a customer storytelling initiative that amplifies mission-aligned customer voices."

### Strategy B: Address a Critical Gap

Find a lens where the company scored poorly and propose an idea that addresses the weakness.

* Tackles the most pressing need
* Could have higher impact if successful
* Shows you understand what's at risk

**Example:** "This company scores low on Governance. My proposal introduces a quarterly stakeholder review process to create accountability."

## Why This Matters

You might think: *"I'm not a founder. I'm not a CEO. What can I actually change?"*

Here's the truth: every organization is shaped by the people inside it. The Culture Bank concept shows that trust is built through small, consistent actions—not grand gestures. The Entry-Level Advantage means your fresh perspective sees things veterans miss.

This week, you practice using your voice. That's a skill that compounds over an entire career.
