## My DEARACT Proposal

### D — Describe: The Facts

> [Write 2–3 sentences describing objective observations from your Module 2 analysis.]

### E — Express: My Perspective

> [Write 2–3 sentences explaining what these findings mean through the Incorruptible framework.]

### A — Assert: My Recommendation

> [Write 1–2 sentences clearly stating your specific proposal.]

### R — Reinforce: Why It Matters

> [Write 2–3 sentences explaining the benefits and why the company should care.]

### A — Align: Connect to Values and Goals

> [Write 1–2 sentences explaining how your proposal connects to the company's stated values, mission, or strategic priorities.]

### C — Clarify: Address Concerns

> [Identify 1–2 potential concerns and explain how you would address them while keeping the proposal focused.]

### T — Together: Invite Collaboration

> [Write 1–2 sentences describing possible alternatives, pilot approaches, or ways to refine the proposal collaboratively.]
