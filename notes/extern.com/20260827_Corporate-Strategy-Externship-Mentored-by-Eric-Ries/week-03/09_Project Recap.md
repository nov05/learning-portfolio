1. **If your project had a highlight reel, what would be on it?**

I found that analyzing a company in a field I already have some experience in allows me to go much deeper.

2. **Which skill do you feel you leveled up the most during this project?**

I became more comfortable looking at a company from the highest level and taking a broader perspective than I normally would.

3. **What was the hardest part — and how did you push through it?**

For some very large or dominant companies, it was hard to find the motivation to analyze them when it felt unlikely that anything could really change. I tried to treat the exercise as a way to understand the company and its system, rather than focusing only on whether my ideas could have an immediate impact.

4. **If you could tweak one part of the project to make it even better, what would you change — and why?**

I think it would be interesting to have more opportunities to discuss the analysis with people from different industries. Different perspectives might reveal things that are easy to miss when looking at a company from within one field.
