```text
Generate a 4-second video using two reference images.
Horizontal 16:9 composition.
Shot with an iPhone 15 Pro Max.
Ultra-realistic photorealistic.

Use REF01 as the reference for the four icon designs and their arrangement. Use REF02 as the reference for the black Chinese calligraphy brushstroke style and flying-white texture.

Use a clean white background with black brushstrokes. Invert the visual style of REF01: do not use a black background or white icon lines.

Create a 4-second animation:
0–1 seconds: Draw the power icon.
1–2 seconds: Draw the arrow icon.
2–3 seconds: Draw the microphone icon.
3–4 seconds: Draw the infinity icon.

The brushstrokes can follow a natural, artistic drawing order. Use expressive black ink, dry-brush texture, irregular white gaps, and varied stroke thickness. Keep each icon recognizable and faithful to the design in REF01.

Preserve the original icon designs and keep all brush texture contained within their intended shapes. Do not embellish or reinterpret the icons.

No music or voiceover. Keep the camera static and the composition horizontal 16:9. By the final frame, all four completed icons should be visible together on the white background.

**Brushstroke texture:** Make the Chinese flying-white (Feibai) effect much more pronounced and clearly visible. Use authentic dry-brush strokes with distinct, irregular white streaks and gaps cutting through the black ink, rough bristle marks, broken ink coverage, and naturally varying stroke thickness. The white gaps must be sharp, visible, and high-contrast, not subtle or blurred. Avoid smooth, solid, uniform vector-like lines.

**Strictly no extra strokes:** Only draw the four icons shown in REF01: power, arrow, microphone, and infinity. Do not add any surrounding circles, loops, rings, spirals, flourishes, borders, decorative strokes, or background brush marks. Every brushstroke must belong directly to one of the four icons. Keep the background completely plain white throughout the video.

**Audio:** Include clearly audible, realistic Chinese calligraphy brush sounds synchronized with each stroke, with a satisfying “shua-shua” dry-brush sound and subtle bristle friction against paper. The brush sounds should be prominent and crisp. No music, no voiceover, and no other sound effects.
```
