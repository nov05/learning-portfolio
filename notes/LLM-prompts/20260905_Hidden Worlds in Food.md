POV of a diner looking down at a traditional Chinese spicy hot pot on a wooden table. The hot pot contains deep crimson-red spicy broth with chili oil, dried chilies, Sichuan peppercorns, tofu, mushrooms, vegetables, and other hot pot ingredients.

STRICT 8-SECOND TIMELINE:

0.0–1.5 seconds — ORIGINAL HOT POT:
Show only the original hot pot scene. The hot pot, spicy broth, ingredients, wooden table, and surrounding environment are clearly visible. Stable POV.

1.5–2.5 seconds — CAMERA PUSH-IN:
The camera slowly and smoothly pushes toward the surface of the spicy broth. The hot pot remains completely recognizable. The camera approaches the broth surface without cutting.

2.5–4.0 seconds — HOT POT → VOLCANIC CAVERN TRANSFORMATION:
The camera enters the broth. The broth progressively fills the entire frame and completely replaces the hot pot scene. The hot pot, table, and outside environment disappear entirely. The liquid transforms continuously into a vast volcanic cavern with deep crimson-red glowing magma, dark charcoal volcanic rock, burgundy shadows, subtle burnt-orange highlights, smoke, and floating embers.

4.0–5.0 seconds — COMPLETE VOLCANIC CAVERN:
Show only the volcanic cavern. No hot pot, no table, no food, and no outside environment are visible. The camera slowly glides through the cavern.

5.0–6.5 seconds — VOLCANIC CAVERN → HOT POT TRANSFORMATION:
The camera retraces its path toward the magma surface. The volcanic cavern progressively collapses back into the liquid. Magma, volcanic rocks, smoke, embers, and every volcanic element completely disappear. The volcanic environment must be fully replaced by the original spicy broth before the hot pot scene returns.

6.5–8.0 seconds — ORIGINAL HOT POT RESTORED:
The camera emerges from the broth and reveals the exact original hot pot scene. The same hot pot, same ingredients, same wooden table, same composition, and same POV are fully restored. No volcanic elements remain anywhere.

IMPORTANT:
Both transformations must be complete. Never show the hot pot and volcanic cavern as two simultaneous environments. Never leave a volcano underneath, behind, or outside the hot pot. During the volcanic phase, only the volcanic world exists. During the final phase, only the original hot pot world exists.
