While I generate text rather than synthesized audio, this passage is a solemn Middle Egyptian invocation to the goddess Isis (*Aset*) using her traditional divine epithets.

**Translation & Epithets**

* **Aset**: Isis (literally "She of the Throne").
* **Aset weret netjeret**: Isis, Great Goddess (*weret* = great, *netjeret* = goddess).
* **Aset mut netjeru**: Isis, Mother of the Gods (*mut* = mother, *netjeru* = gods).
* **Nebet pet**: Lady of Heaven (*nebet* = mistress/lady, *pet* = sky/heaven).
* **Nesut netjeru**: Ruler/Queen of the Gods (*nesut* = monarch/ruler).
* **Sekhem Aset**: Power/Might of Isis (*sekhem* = spiritual power or vital force).

If you are crafting this prompt for a text-to-speech (TTS) engine like ElevenLabs, Bark, or Audiobox, inserting phonetic breaks (e.g., *Ah-set, WEH-ret, NET-jeh-ret*) can help maintain that deliberate, authoritative cadence and prevent the model from mispronouncing the transliterated Egyptian words.
